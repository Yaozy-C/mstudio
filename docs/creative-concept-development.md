# Creative concept development: research and exploratory check

## Change

The October 2026 concept experiments produced more detailed proposals without consistently producing stronger central ideas. The maintained correction is in `skills/creative-concepts`: new directions now load `references/discovery-and-development.md` before expanding an execution. The concept role still supplies a premise and its advertising meaning; screenplay, shot design and production stay with their existing roles.

The guide adds ways to inspect concrete material, transfer a relationship rather than its costume, change a dependency, discover an overlooked participant and explore meaning from an image or sound as well as from a proposition. It asks the author to discover the audience's attraction and develop the next consequence of the premise before adding execution detail. These are generative options and normal selection judgments, not a new review service, scoring threshold, fixed story formula or extra approval round. Ordinary, sensory and quiet work remain valid.

## Research basis

- [IDEO U: analogous inspiration](https://www.ideou.com/blogs/inspiration/activity-explore-analogous-inspiration) grounds transfer in the behavior behind an experience.
- [SIT: systematic creativity discussions](https://www.sitsite.com/tag/systematic-creativity/) explore changing arrangements and roles of existing elements. The guide adapts these to expressive fiction, without treating invented product capabilities as facts.
- [D&AD's Canal+ / BETC interview](https://www.dandad.org/insights/awards/bear-case-study-insights) describes developing a particular observer's relationship to cinema into a character. [Saatchi & Saatchi's Tide account](https://saatchi.com/en-us/news/tide-wins-the-super-clio-for-best-ad-campaign-of-the-super-bowl) illustrates a category fact becoming a rule for interpreting the surrounding medium.
- The guide also points to published HomePod and Nike case descriptions and historical advertising-template research. Source access was written accounts and interviews, not a new full-film and audio audit. These methods and cases do not establish AI-video performance.

## Exploratory check on 20 October 2026

Baseline: the repository concept package at commit `391da473e35e00afb44dff8b6cf59c1d790b3d82`, whose entry matched the installed database document at revision 16. Independent agents used the same gray 25L lunch-bag brief, original two images, language, duration and fact restrictions. Each actually loaded its assigned package and inspected both images. No model override, web research, media generation or live-project editing was part of the exercise.

One baseline run produced three concepts; two revised-package runs produced six. All nine descriptions, without author, source, recommendation or explanatory rationale, were shuffled and supplied to two independent review agents. Both prioritized the revised package's portable-noon and reluctant-furniture concepts. Both also identified weak development after their strongest initial actions. The authors were asked for a subsequent concept revision; these revised endings were not part of the original blind comparison.

This was an exploratory iteration, not a balanced experiment or an estimated win rate. The revised package had twice as many candidates; there was one main product, one configured model and model-based reviewers with related preferences. Familiar music and self-care ideas still recurred. The results support further development of particular premises, not a claim that the skill reliably meets the reference-ad standard.

A separate text-only transparent-umbrella exercise used a fictional brief and no product images. It checked whether the guide could be used outside the lunch-bag task; it had no baseline comparison and provides no evidence of real-product accuracy or general quality improvement.

## Continuity follow-up

The portable-noon revision added a colleague joining the protagonist for lunch. Although thematically consistent, that continuation stopped developing the visual relationship that made the premise interesting. The subsequent correction expands the existing development section: distinguish a consequence of the premise from an additional scene, recover what the audience has already discovered, and explore implications, changed relationships or variations before supplying a generic emotional resolution. A fictional mannequin exercise illustrates the distinction without prescribing an advertisement.

The concept entry now routes selected-premise extensions to that guidance and carries the essential attraction into the writer handoff. The screenwriting entry also preserves the attraction as well as the message. These changes do not forbid secondary characters, friendship or sentiment, require a twist, or turn quiet/sensory work into plot. They do not grant the concept author screenplay or production permissions.

Independent follow-up agents extended the same selected portable-noon premise with the preceding and revised packages. Both continued the calendar/lunch relationship; a source-blind reviewer called the comparison a tie, with repetition and prop-rule weaknesses in both. A separate writer used the revised screenwriting entry to extend the fictional rain-as-acquaintance umbrella premise into a 25-second script, retaining that relationship throughout. This checks a specific continuity behavior; it neither reproduces the earlier failure reliably nor demonstrates a quality advantage for the revision. The two metadata validators, package/link checks and 15 existing skill-related tests pass.

## Creative-strength follow-up

The next research pass focused on the quality of a premise rather than its continuity. The guide now asks authors to explore a viewpoint alongside the device, recognize the category's automatic advertisement, and judge the concrete recognition, character, image or sensation before the explanatory pitch. A particular attitude can emerge from ordinary life; a transformed object or abstract quality is not automatically an interesting idea. Sensory work is evaluated through its actual motion, scale, texture, pattern and sound relationship, rather than an imagined premium execution.

Imported written cases add three different kinds of attraction: [IKEA's acknowledgment of parents' precedence](https://www.wpp.com/en/case-studies/ogilvy-david-and-ingo-al-futtaim-ikeas-proudly-second-best), [Harvey Nichols' reinterpretation of festive gifting](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/sorry-i-spent-it-on-myself), and [Sony/Fallon's color-and-motion premise](https://www.dandad.org/insights/awards/sony-balls-case-study-insights). These illustrate choices, not recipes to copy, and do not establish current audience preferences.

The exploratory protocol uses two briefs (the real gray lunch bag and a fictional transparent umbrella) with three independent conditions: the preceding package, the revised package and no Skill. Each condition produces two concepts per brief, twelve test concepts total. Descriptions are anonymized without authors' recommendations or rationales. Two independent model reviewers consider actual viewing attraction, freshness of interpretation and sensory potential; they distinguish a relative preference from a distinctly compelling concept. Two published-campaign summaries from separate products/formats provide qualitative calibration, not matched controls or a test of finished-film quality. Recognizable references and related model preferences remain sources of bias. One run per condition cannot establish a general quality or causal effect.

That candidate (v5) did not produce a favorable result. Both reviewers prioritized the same preceding-package and no-Skill concepts; neither shortlisted a revised-package concept. One reviewer considered the preceding package's droplet sports commentary distinctive enough to develop; the other found no distinctly compelling test concept. Both distinguished a few enjoyable moments from a strong central attraction. This provides no evidence of improvement and does not establish that the candidate caused deterioration. The v5 candidate was not applied to the installed database.

The next candidate replaces abstract viewpoint reminders with concrete premise-generation moves: an unusually interested participant, competing desires, an unreasonable alternative and a precise sensory relationship, alongside the existing relationship transformations. These are optional ways to generate material, not a fixed genre or demand for a bizarre character. It asks authors to compare short observable studies before full narratives and separates the studies' attraction from their explanations. This adapts the historical template research at the level of expressive relationships; it does not authorize stronger product claims.

A fresh three-condition exercise gives every author the same six short studies per product, then two developed concepts per product. Thus the candidate receives no extra candidate allowance. The preceding live package and a no-Skill condition receive the same brief, images, fact limits and output count. The exercise is exploratory, with a single author per condition and model reviewers. It cannot estimate a reliable win rate or establish finished-film quality.

In this second exercise (v6), both source-blind reviewers prioritized the candidate's transparent-umbrella premise in which the walker composes changing city views as an overhead ceiling, ending with a streetlight aligned as a rain-covered chandelier. They pointed to that actual visual relationship, while questioning the added stranger's smile and the untested readability of the image. One prioritized the candidate's missing-beat food-sound premise; the other preferred its competing-appetites premise only relatively and considered it ordinary. Both also prioritized different no-Skill umbrella concepts. Neither reviewer called any test concept distinctly compelling. The v6 candidate therefore has promising material to develop, not a demonstrated general quality advantage or evidence of reaching the original advertisements' standard.

A separate source-blind reviewer saw all 36 short studies without the complete proposals. Its priorities included the candidate's discarded courthouse-wedding lunch and its umbrella ceiling, and the no-Skill author's self-reviewing cook and father/child disguise. This suggests that selection can discard promising material as well as generation failing to produce it; one review cannot establish which choice an audience would prefer. The promising wedding study is not a tested, finished concept. No new rule requiring a wedding, unusual character, twist or sensory film is inferred from these preferences.

The maintained update is v6's concrete exploration guidance and the useful case notes, with the negative and mixed results retained here. The revision is an openly provisional method update. It is not a quality certification, a claim that model reviewers establish taste, or a reason to skip trying the actual image, sound and performance. The rejected v5 package and both exercises' full texts and judgments are preserved with the user-facing experiment report.
