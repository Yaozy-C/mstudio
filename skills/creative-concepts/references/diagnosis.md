# Different ways a TikTok advertising idea can arise

Use these notes to understand creative connections, not copy topics or impose a common story shape. They describe specific work and its conditions. Creative interpretations below are working deductions; published results do not establish that a particular script element caused sales. Mstudio has no network search: these notes are imported evidence, and linked pages are sources rather than required live tool calls.

## Doritos: a desirable sensation has a social consequence

[PS21's campaign account](https://www.ps21.works/case/doritos-mas-crujientes-mas-molestos) starts with a reformulated, crunchier chip. It turns the sound into an interruption: a cinema encounter with Lola Lolita, followed by an ASMR reveal and other executions juxtaposing the crunch with annoying sounds. [TikTok's awards account](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/gala-y-ganadores-premios-tiktok-awards-2025?lang=es) identifies the TikTok-first campaign. The agency's video compilation is [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsbB-v10hEc).

Creative connection: follow a real quality into a context that changes its meaning. The pleasant sensation for one person becomes an interruption for another. The interpretation supplies dramatic material beyond explaining the recipe. This does not require conflict in every ad or legitimise fabricated public controversy presented as real. Access: campaign text and sampled compilation frames; no usable transcript or direct audio assessment.

## Currys: language means something different beside a product

[iSite's production page](https://isite.solutions/our-work/currys/socials) publishes the Gen-Z-script video and explains adapting phrases, sounds and other platform material. [Currys' own interview](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/currysplc_many-many-people-are-dreaming-of-an-interview-activity-7245064390897541121-v5CN) says a real-estate video supplied the initial inspiration. The [original film](https://video.isitetv.com/video/upload/f_auto/Gen_Z_V2_100mb_n4mqgs.mp4) puts a uniformed store colleague beside appliances while he delivers youth slang. A microwave accompanies language about eating; the closing returns-policy passage plays on having receipts.

Creative connection: the words, speaker and objects produce a second reading together. This is more specific than instructing a writer to sound casual. The original is about a minute long and represents a retailer; neither its feature coverage nor its language is a mandatory approach for a short single-product ad. Access: local audio transcription and sampled frames; slang in the machine transcript is imperfect, and it does not establish vocal nuance.

## Currys: the difficulty of performing the ad becomes the action

The same producer publishes a [Samsung gifting short](https://video.isitetv.com/video/upload/f_auto/Samsung_Laptop_Gifting_2_kkshji.mp4). It begins with a physical clip, then two store colleagues repeatedly stumble or interrupt attempts to deliver laptop copy before a clear product message is completed. The laptop stays visible during the performance. Access: sampled frames and machine transcription of the approximately 27-second film; some early fragments are unclear.

Creative connection: the performance situation can supply the entertainment while the product message remains legible. This is a counterexample to requiring every action to be physically caused by a feature. It is not evidence that unrelated opening clips improve conversion, nor a recipe to add interruptions to every script.

## KFC: audience language meets the brand's own materials

[TikTok's award account](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-GB/blog/tiktokadawardsuk24-winners-blog) describes responding to the audience's “oil up” prompt with cooking oil and KFC's herbs-and-spices identity. Teasers and comment replies preceded the main content. [The agency's published work](https://lbbonline.com/work/124794) documents the campaign.

Creative connection: a phrase from the audience and a recognisable part of the product world acquire a shared, literal interpretation. The idea can begin outside the feature list and still become specific to the advertiser. This depended on actual community context and brand recognition; do not invent comments, audience demand or continuing currency for an old trend. Access: published campaign descriptions, not complete audiovisual observation.

## IKEA: a physical preference provides unusually specific material

[Tom Watts' first-person account](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-i-created-tiktok-first-spec-commercial-ikeas-tillagd-tom-watts-cwl9e) describes developing a 15-second TILLAGD-spoon spec film from his earlier Spoon Council content and sensory preferences. The described expression includes manipulating the spoon and a looping sequence. This was a self-initiated spec, not a verified commissioned IKEA campaign; earlier-post engagement is creator-reported and not a sales result.

Creative connection: close attention to how an object feels or is handled can reveal material overlooked by a generic benefit statement. Sensory specificity is available alongside comic or narrative invention. Do not transfer the author's unsupported universal claims about logos, UGC trust or guaranteed viewing. Access: first-person written account only.

## Nutter Butter: recognisable product assets sustain a fictional world

[Dentsu's account](https://www.dentsu.com/us/en/our-work/nutter-butter-you-good) describes an ongoing TikTok world of surreal characters, unfinished narratives and audience participation, with the cookie continually central. The work sought renewed cultural relevance for an established brand.

Creative connection: distinctive product form and established identity can support invented entertainment beyond a literal usage demonstration. Its accumulated lore and recognisable product are important conditions. An unknown item in a standalone sales ad still needs intelligible identity and value; surreal content is not automatically a reason to buy it. Access: agency account, not full viewing of the series.

## Apply the relationships, not the surfaces

The useful abstraction differs across these cases: a sensory quality with social consequences; language and an object sharing meanings; a performance situation; audience participation; a physical preference; or a recognisable product becoming fictional material. None alone defines TikTok creativity. Work from the current product, people and available material to discover a connection of your own.

A functional fact can lead to an idea, but immediately choosing one function and one ordinary scene can close off the other material. Conversely, entertaining people about something unrelated does not communicate the offer. Develop both the invention and its product meaning while the premise is still rough, before committing the whole script to it.

## Turn inspiration into generative work

In [her first-person strategy interview](https://lbbonline.com/news/planning-for-the-best-cecelia-parrish-on-why-strategy-and-creative-should-work-hand-in-hand), Havas Chicago's Cecelia Parrish describes clarifying who communication should move and what problem it addresses, supplying rich material for creatives, and connecting the work to product truth. She also describes strategy changing alongside creative development. This supports a focused communication question with freedom in its expression, rather than treating every product detail as an equally useful proposition or imposing one narrative sequence. This is practitioner experience, not causal performance evidence.

[IDEO's brainstorming practice](https://www.ideou.com/blogs/inspiration/7-simple-rules-of-brainstorming) separates producing many rough ideas from early criticism and explicitly welcomes unusual suggestions. Its [analogous-inspiration exercise](https://www.ideou.com/blogs/inspiration/activity-explore-analogous-inspiration) looks outside the immediate context for a shared experience or feeling, examines the specific behaviour behind it, and uses that relationship to generate new possibilities. These are general creative-development methods, not TikTok performance evidence.

For this Skill, the practical adaptation is to make a page of short, concrete premises before selecting a screenplay. Bring distinct observations together, let rough connections develop, and postpone the effort of polishing one execution. The suggested six to ten sketches is a working scope for a short assignment, not a number established by the sources or a success threshold. The resulting proposition should remain relevant to what the advertiser can actually offer. The method supplies work to do before the first draft; it adds no scoring or post-draft review stage.
