# Creative concepts

Create an idea that makes a relevant product truth interesting to experience. Commercial relevance and imaginative expression develop together. The feature, audience problem and familiar usage scene are possible starting material, not a prescribed order or a finished concept.

## Open the brief before narrowing it

Carry the actual audience, market, language, purpose, duration and user choices. Distinguish those from earlier agents' suggestions. Read original product material. Notice its full usable range: construction, appearance, handling, capabilities, combinations, context and supplied brand assets. These facts are creative material, not equally important selling propositions.

Establish the advertising job: what is the audience being invited to choose, and which meaningful difference from its ordinary alternative makes that choice worthwhile? Carry a category promise or purchasing concern named in the brief into this question. Supporting details can enrich the answer, but do not silently replace that central promise with a more easily pictured specification. A qualitative capability remains usable at its supported scope even when exact measurements are unavailable. The question focuses what the ad should mean; it does not prescribe a scene, problem–solution plot or physical demonstration.

Gather concrete material from people's behaviour and the world around the product: conventions, rituals, desires, social frictions, phrases, visual associations, buying questions or sensory details. The material may come from research or a clearly fictional observation. A broad demographic supplies little specificity. Mstudio has no network search capability; use supplied or imported evidence. For TikTok work, read the [creative case notes](references/diagnosis.md) for different ways actual work connects these materials.

Keep confirmed facts, interpretation and invention distinct. Use qualitative capabilities at their supported scope; do not invent specifications, measured outcomes, purchase histories or customer quotes. These limits define what the ad can claim, not how literally it must depict the idea. Ask only for missing information that changes the assignment materially.

## Make a page of ideas before writing a script

For an open creative brief, develop a small page of roughly six to ten short premises before expanding any into a screenplay. These are working sketches, not six finished scripts or a review of a completed draft. Keep each to an image, exchange or event and the product meaning it expresses. Explore different answers and expressions within the advertising job, rather than abandoning the job to obtain novelty. Make room for unfinished, strange and visually simple ideas before deciding which one to develop. Deliver only the requested number of works; provide the short concept sketches separately when requested.

Build this material from particulars. Put observations about this object beside observations about people, language and other parts of life. Describe what happens in each, rather than attaching broad words such as convenience or confidence. Look beyond the category's usual places and routines for a parallel behaviour, relationship, convention or sensation. Borrow the underlying relationship and transform it with the product; merely dressing an ordinary use demonstration in another setting does not develop the analogy. Invented observations are creative material, not research findings.

Move between these materials while sketching. Follow an implication somewhere unexpected, give an action a different interpretation, connect a phrase to an object's behaviour, or compose a visual relationship that says something words alone would not. Develop different thoughts about the offer before making variations of one execution. A different cast, place, food or genre around the same thought is an execution variation. There is no assigned set of topics or formats to fill. A useful discovery or direct demonstration can also supply an idea.

Develop the commercial thought inside each promising sketch: what becomes better, possible or worth choosing through this product, relative to the audience's existing behaviour or alternative? Write that thought plainly in the working material, then express it through the invented event. Keep the central buying reason active in that event; a playful treatment of an incidental detail should not take its place. It need not become a spoken comparison or claim of exclusive superiority. A familiar-category product with no established brand story needs its actual category value or meaningful design to do some work; an appealing character and the product's name alone do not supply that value. Relevance still does not require every action to be physically caused by a feature or every film to prove performance.

Choose the sketch that makes a relevant thought feel newly interesting, rather than automatically choosing the most familiar, realistic or easiest-to-explain scene. Develop the interesting leap into a legible piece; do not retreat to a routine illustration simply because its connection is more immediate. Preserve truthful claims and the brief's actual production limits. An unfamiliar item in a standalone ad and an established brand's serial entertainment have different recognition to draw on. Do not import a famous brand's audience, assets or campaign conditions.

## Develop the chosen idea as a piece

Let the premise determine the form, speaker, amount of explanation and audiovisual rhythm. A familiar TikTok convention can be useful when its actual language or behaviour connects to this product. A trend label, casual slang or popular sound alone is not that connection. Use documented current trends when claiming currency; otherwise invent self-contained material.

Compose the decisive action and wording, then develop their implications, variation, discovery or payoff. The viewer may enjoy an unfolding process, recognise a behaviour, follow an argument or understand a joke. Whatever supplies the interest should remain active through the piece. Do not turn the middle into a compulsory mechanism explanation or feature tour.

A narrator may be expressive, ironic, matter-of-fact or absent. Dialogue, sound, captions and images can add different parts of the meaning. Invent the actual significant words and events now; keep technical production choices for direction. Fiction and stylisation are available, while factual promises remain grounded. No required problem–solution arc, suspense, reversal or CTA is implied.

## Deliver and hand off

Briefly state the premise and the product truth it communicates. Preserve the actual opening, development, ending, decisive wording, objects and sound relationships; an abstract benefit summary loses the invention. Carry consequential claim limits as writing context rather than filling the film with caveats.

Continue to screenwriting when a complete script was requested. Deliver the requested work without a separate approval, score or self-critique stage. Concepts remain conversation content unless a supported save is requested. This Skill does not author timed screenplay or shot records, model prompts, media or timelines, and grants no additional permissions.
