# Still scene execution

Execute the selected design as one visible instant. Return changes to the premise, shot purpose or essential camera relationship to the responsible role with the specific conflict; do not redesign the story while preparing an image.

## Viewpoint and visibility

State where the camera is relative to the subject, what it looks toward, its height and distance, and which surfaces must be visible. An interior view looking out through an opening differs from an exterior view looking into it. Establish near edges, occlusion and perspective that make the position readable. Light enters through an actual opening or comes from an explicitly motivated source; brightness does not by itself prove camera location.

Retain the selected attention center, silhouette, subject/target relationship and usable negative space. A wide lens does not permit mutually incompatible surfaces. Keep support, contact and hand placement plausible in the chosen moment. Anticipation, transit and a settled result are different states; do not merge them in a still. Preserve required visibility and no-face constraints, including reflections.

## Photographic appearance

Describe the scene's motivated light direction, size, softness and contrast, then the visible material response. Skin, cloth, metal and translucent plastic respond differently. Preserve useful texture at the actual subject scale, with believable highlights and shadow contact. Avoid stacking generic photorealism adjectives or adding microscopic skin detail to a distant figure. Reference lighting for identity need not become the scene lighting.

## Image constraints and local repairs

Original product references define the visible construction. Preserve every required visible component, its count, location and connection, not just color and silhouette. Perspective and occlusion may hide a part; do not invent hidden construction or move parts to make props fit. Keep frame state, proportions, lighting, hands, contact and crop consistent; a material edit must preserve the other relationships.

For realism judge integrated anatomy, light, material, scale and support rather than surface sharpness alone. Organize inputs by identity, scene, composition and actual edit base. Edit a valid base for local corrections; regenerate only when that cannot preserve the needed relationships. A local edit must preserve unrelated regions and stable relationships. Task creation is not completion. A static image cannot establish continuous motion or sound.
