# Photographic people and environmental light

Use when the brief calls for photographic people or live-action scenes. Keep the requested art direction, location, ethnicity, age, body type and skin tone. Realism does not imply a fixed palette, lens, geography or beauty standard. Describe the visible cause of an appearance; do not stack "cinematic", "8K" or "dermatological" claims. Resolution belongs in supported parameters.

## Skin and detail

At the visible scale keep irregular texture, fine lines and restrained local colour variation. Close views may show subtle vellus hair and micro-shadowing, with localised oil sheen on raised facial areas rather than a plastic or waxy surface. Match skin detail to focus and distance; keep intentional makeup, age marks and styling. Sharpness supports natural optical detail without halos, etched pores or repeated noise. A catchlight agrees with the light source.

## Light

Specify motivated sources, direction, softness, exposure relationships and the relevant room or weather cues. Optional setups to adapt, never defaults:

- Interior day: window light as principal source, a warmer practical lamp only if present; readable facial modelling and shadow detail. Off-white, pale wood, grey-blue and light brown make one restrained palette.
- Interior night: practical lamps motivate low illumination, with weak cooler window ambience where plausible; keep shadow separation without turning night into day. Mood comes from the story, not the hour.
- Overcast exterior: broad soft skylight, gentle contrast, optional humidity or haze supported by the weather; grey-blue, grey-green, dry green and light brown with plausible skin.
- Soft sunny exterior: softened or bounced daylight with a clear direction, controlled highlights and readable shadows; keep the real weather and time, and keep a deliberately hard-sun design when chosen.

For an explicitly chosen restrained realist look: low-to-moderate saturation and contrast, slight warm and cool separation, gentle highlight diffusion, restrained halation and fine organic grain. Apply only what serves the reference; grain is not skin texture, and diffusion must not hide required detail. A 35mm or 50mm perspective is a choice, not a rule. Keep the required environment and spatial relationships legible.

Carry the palette, source relationships and degree of diffusion consistently across related shots, adapting to justified changes of location or time. Correct visible plastic skin, over-sharpening or lost background with a targeted fix, not by appending a full negative list to every request.
