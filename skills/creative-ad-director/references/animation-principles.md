# Animation principles as visible performance

Use for action design, motion review, or a pose or contact problem. Pick the principles that improve this shot's purpose, material and style; never append all twelve as a slogan.

| Principle | Apply through visible decisions |
|---|---|
| Squash and stretch | Specify load, deformation, timing and recovery to convey weight or elasticity. Rigid real products do not stretch; soft objects respond to support and material. Exaggeration keeps identity and structure recognisable. |
| Anticipation | Prepare a decisive action with a weight shift, a reverse motion, loading or attention. A surprise may omit preparation on purpose; an omitted cause is a different thing. Avoid a wind-up before every action. |
| Staging | Make the main action legible through view, silhouette, pose, occlusion and sequencing; contact and result must read. See [shot grammar](cinematography.md). |
| Straight ahead and pose to pose | Design the key poses that precise causality or contact needs; let fluids and smoke evolve. Specify start, decisive poses and result without an image for every pose or pretending frame-level control exists. |
| Follow through and overlap | Say when the body and attached parts respond; describe lag and settling. No simultaneous stops, no detached floating attachments. |
| Slow in and slow out | Choose speed curves for initiation, effort, impact and recovery. Impacts may stop abruptly; mechanisms may move uniformly. Universal smooth easing makes action soft. |
| Arcs | Follow joints, pivots and inertia where natural; keep constrained mechanisms straight. Arcs never justify penetration or a moving pivot. |
| Secondary action | Support the main action, emotion or speed with subordinate gestures and environmental response, in a stated order, not simultaneous noise. |
| Timing | Allocate time by mass, distance, force and reading needs; separate completion, recognition and reaction. Equal divisions and model limits never erase a required beat. |
| Exaggeration | Name the pose, speed contrast or reaction that is amplified and keep the other relationships. Exaggeration is never product proof. |
| Solid drawing | Keep perspective, weight, support, volume and occlusion through every pose. Rich texture does not compensate for weightlessness, penetration or missing parts. |
| Appeal | Readable silhouettes, pose rhythm, contrast and character. Appeal is not cute, symmetrical or polished; under a no-face rule it lives in body, hands, objects and timing. |

## Turn principles into beats

Use the phases the shot needs: initial state or motive, effort or start, decisive contact or change, result, and recognition or reaction. Phases may merge or be omitted on purpose, but keep the causality, joke or product evidence that depends on them. For each relevant point, say what the camera sees and when it follows, stops or cuts; subject speed and camera speed are separate.

For each chosen principle, specify the affected object, the trigger, the change in path, pose or speed, its order relative to the main action, and the observable acceptance point. Weight comes from material-specific response, not a universal bounce.

Reaching the endpoint and delivering a performance are different outcomes: a gag that needs a recognition pause fails without it. Inspect staging, poses and attention in stills; inspect timing, speed, weight and reaction in playback.

## Inputs and diagnosis

Motion references constrain action and timing; appearance references constrain identity and material; the two must be compatible. If a rough proxy hides the interaction, add spatial and contact cues; if a detailed proxy is being copied as appearance, strip the irrelevant styling or change the route.

Diagnose the specific cause: missing action source, misplaced attention, wrong key pose, wrong speed curve, missing reaction, or conflicting reference appearance. Repair that design or input. Do not append "natural, lively, powerful" or speed the whole clip up. Current retry, budget and authorisation limits stay in force.

For live-action acting and interaction, read [naturalistic performance](naturalistic-performance.md).
