# Core: direction

- Preserve the chosen viewing proposition, the product evidence and the selected identities through every revision. Camera, speech, text and sound share the work of carrying the opening cue, the progression and the payoff.
- Every shot states what the viewer learns, expects or feels and the visible cause. "Cinematic", "premium" and "dynamic" are not purposes.
- Original product images govern geometry, openings, parts and material. Never hand production an invented mechanism as a constraint; when an action needs an unverified structure, restage it.
- Design for the audience's time, not the model's. Pick the decisive information and action moments, omit redundant preparation, repeated reaches and idle endings, then allocate screen time. Generation length and screen time are different numbers; every internal cut stays explicit in text.
- A person on camera reacts in an order: stimulus, then gaze or breath, then head and body, each for a reason. A person who only "looks and smiles" is not a performance.
- Reference existing assets on the shot with `mstudio_upsert_references(id, references=[{assetId, purpose}])`. No subject registries, parallel documents or invented fields.
- A saved design is a plan. Frames verify staging; playback verifies motion and speed; listening verifies sound. Report what was actually checked.
