# Core: direction

Own the viewing experience and shooting decisions: framing, viewpoint, lens/distance, focus, staging, performance, movement and cuts. Carry the chosen premise through its opening, progression and payoff. Give production original asset IDs and relevant product facts; image and video production own reference binding, visual identity fidelity and generated repairs. Preserve factual boundaries without turning each shot into a product inventory or forcing the same visible faces, angle or scale throughout. Different views may change projection and occlusion while depicting the same product.

Separate the shot's action from the instant chosen for its frame. Identify only the state and spatial relationships that affect the event, reveal or cut. Do not invent an essential mechanism or contradictory action. Production realizes detailed contact, support and material behavior and corrects execution drift; a different design is needed only when the design itself conflicts with the facts or intended experience. See [state continuity](references/continuity.md). For a new shooting approach, use [shot grammar](references/cinematography.md), including lens/distance and focus; varied focal lengths or angles are choices, not quotas.

Design for the audience's time, not the model's. Choose distinct information and decisive action moments, leave out redundant preparation, repeated reaches and idle endings, and only then allocate screen time. State intended film timing and planned cuts. Production chooses supported generation durations; editing selects the actual source intervals. Preserve the meaning of any existing generation-group record rather than converting it into a film-duration field.

Save the Mstudio production plan as generation-unit shot records of at least 5 seconds, following [shot-record timing](SKILL.md#mstudio-shot-records-and-timing). Group adjacent, narratively compatible editorial shots in one record, put the whole unit span in shot.duration, and preserve every internal shot's full description, exact time range and designed cut in its text. Internal shots may last fractions of a second; do not round them up or stretch the film. Separate short records with separate 5-second generation tasks do not satisfy the grouping requirement. Direction saves the grouped design; production adapts it to supported model limits.

For any human beat, decide the trigger, how gaze, breath and body respond and overlap according to that stimulus, the purpose the action serves right now, and what it connects to. Their order is not fixed. Specify the behavior that carries the intended meaning.

Detailed methods, task triggers and the boundaries between Skills are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

Hand off editorial shots, timings and useful representative moments. Image production maps them into the selected storyboard deliverable; do not infer its cell count or asset count from the number of editorial shots. Preserve the user's chosen full-reference workflow without adding start/end controls.
