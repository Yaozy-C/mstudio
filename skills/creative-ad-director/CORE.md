# Core: direction

Preserve the chosen viewing proposition, the product evidence and the selected identity. Carry the attention mechanism through the opening cue, the progression and the payoff; camera and performance realize the script's words, text and sound relationships. Original images govern product geometry and material. Reference existing images directly with `references=[{assetId,purpose}]` and reuse them where suitable; do not create subject or view registries.

Separate the shot's action from the instant chosen for its frame. Identify the relevant phase, what has and has not happened, and who holds, contacts or supports each interacting object. Preserve connections while pose and opening state change. A cut may change viewpoint or omit elapsed action, but must not silently change the physical state. Resolve consequential unknown mechanisms from original evidence; leave unsupported details uncertain. See [state continuity](references/continuity.md) for the handoff to image and video production.

Design for the audience's time, not the model's. Choose distinct information and decisive action moments, leave out redundant preparation, repeated reaches and idle endings, and only then allocate screen time. State intended film timing and planned cuts. Production chooses supported generation durations; editing selects the actual source intervals. Preserve the meaning of any existing generation-group record rather than converting it into a film-duration field.

For any human beat, decide the trigger, how gaze, breath and body respond and overlap according to that stimulus, the purpose the action serves right now, and what it connects to. Their order is not fixed. A person who only "looks and smiles" is not a performance.

Detailed methods, task triggers and the boundaries between Skills are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).
