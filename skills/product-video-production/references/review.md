# Review actual output and deliver

Recover the current requirements, the failure conditions and the locked content. You inspect the actual media; this is not per-shot user approval. Record findings per [inspection evidence](evidence.md), not as a restatement of the prompt.

## Image, action and sequence

Check identity, structure, colour, visible components, anatomy, counts, reflections and project framing restrictions against the original sources. Check contact and release, openings, paths, support, occlusion and deformation across the actual motion. Check props, openings, outfit, geography, light, attention and cuts across shots. Creative exaggeration follows the selected plan; unexempted physical and product relationships still need coherence.

A wrong product, a fabricated function, a forbidden face, disconnected anatomy, decisive penetration, duplicated items or a missing essential event blocks acceptance; repair the affected material within the current retry limits. Record minor irrelevant deviations without claiming perfection.

For transfers, grasps, opening and closing, occlusion or loss of support, inspect before, contact and after as continuously as the tools allow, using dense frames as an aid; sparse overview frames cannot establish brief contact. Verify that the planned cuts and matching views actually appear. A correct destination cannot cancel a wrong origin: trace the first appearance and support backward, then inspect the chain forward. Compare the actual shots with the plan for omitted events and extra repetition.

## Playback and technical checks

Play the actual new export for information order, required reveal times, cuts, speed and holds; listen for sync, clipping and level. Stills and metadata support only their own scope; record unavailable playback or audio as unchecked, not passed. A new export or a retimed version needs fresh checks of the affected content.

Verify that the file opens and its duration, dimensions, frame rate, audio and codec when tools allow. An aspect conversion must not distort the product or remove evidence. Check platform safe areas only when relevant. Inspect the final file, not only the edit.

For realistic work judge [perceptual realism](generation.md#perceptual-realism) separately from structure. For [visible components](generation.md#visible-product-components) record the original region, the expected visibility and the actual observation; a missing expected part fails, unreadable evidence stays unchecked.

## Traceable delivery

Keep the actual prompts and parameters, input roles and order, task IDs, assets, selected ranges, timeline and repair history in the task's own records, without secrets. Track dependencies and inspect only the affected assets after a base changes.

Show a usable video and concise specifications and changes with real accessible paths. Distinguish a continuous-action film, a still animatic and unfinished assets. Do not deliver only a ZIP, a prompt or an API success status. A known essential error cannot be renamed a resolution limit or an unverified item. Missing audience metrics do not block an authorised delivery; complete records do not themselves prove correct media.
