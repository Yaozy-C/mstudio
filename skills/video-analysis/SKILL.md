# Original video analysis

Work only from the original video and the user's question. No tools, project lookup, asset search, research, generation or project writes are needed. The application supplies this Skill directly. Deliver the breakdown in the conversation.

## Observe before explaining

Identify what was actually accessible: video, audio, subtitles, duration and temporal detail. If no original video was supplied, request it. A filename, description, transcript or set of stills is not the original video. Do not fill missing footage or sound from expectations. When a component is unavailable, analyze the accessible parts and name the gap.

Record meaningful time ranges in source-video time. Use approximate timestamps when precision is uncertain; do not invent frame accuracy. Group by a change in action, information or purpose rather than forcing a fixed number of shots. Distinguish a cut from camera movement and a continuous action from an editorial omission.

For each relevant range, describe visible subjects, actions, composition and camera behavior; exact readable onscreen text; audible speech, music and effects; and the new information or feeling the range contributes. Keep spoken words, subtitles and interpretation separate. Quote speech only when heard clearly; mark uncertain or inaudible words. Do not infer listening from visible mouth movement or complete playback from sampled frames.

## Explain the mechanism

Explain what expectation the opening creates, how the video develops it, what resolves or changes, and the product's actual role when a product is present. Identify how image, speech, silence, music, editing and repetition work together. Tie each interpretation to an observed moment. Distinguish observation from a plausible explanation of viewer response; popularity, conversion and audience intent cannot be established from the video alone.

Analyze people, demonstrations, sensory processes or stories as the source presents them. Do not force every video into a pain-point advertisement, a hook template or a sales funnel. Note unresolved expectations and contradictions when present, without repairing the source into a better imaginary video.

## Deliver the requested depth

For a general breakdown, give a short description of the video's central event, a compact table of time / picture and action / speech and text / sound / beat purpose, then the decisive structural and pacing observations. Combine columns when the source is simple. For a specific question, focus on the relevant moments instead of repeating a full report.

A breakdown ends with understanding the source. Do not propose replacement dialogue, a new concept, an adaptation, shot-production instructions or model prompts unless the user requests a separate creative task; explain that such work belongs to the corresponding creative role. Do not search for product facts or claim that an AI-rendered demonstration proves actual product performance. Do not claim saved records, approval, playback coverage or commercial results beyond the available evidence.
