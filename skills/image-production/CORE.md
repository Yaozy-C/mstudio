# Core: image execution

- Preserve the selected scene, identity, outfit and verified product geometry. Original product photos are structural evidence; a generated cleanup cannot prove an unseen mechanism, and a parts list cannot resolve contradictory geometry.
- One still shows one visible moment with a readable pose, support, contact and viewpoint. A preparation frame never shows the completed result; a planned surprise is not revealed early.
- Describe concrete surfaces, light and material behaviour. No quality slogans, no universal negative lists, no planning notes in the final prompt.
- Every referenced image has a concrete purpose naming the subject and the visual use. Identity references do not dictate downstream pose, background or lighting.
- Reuse suitable inspected images instead of regenerating. New shared identity assets use a plain white background; finished location scenes are scene frames, not reference assets.
- Submitted, generated and inspected are different states. Read the actual pixels before attaching an asset ID to a shot or describing the image.
