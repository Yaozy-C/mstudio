# Core: image execution

Preserve the selected scene, identity, outfit and verified product geometry. One still shows one visible moment with a readable pose, support, contact and viewpoint; a preparation frame must not show a completed result. Use concrete surfaces and lighting instead of generic polish or quality slogans.

Reuse suitable real images rather than regenerating them. New shared identity references use a plain white background unless the user asked for another asset deliverable; finished location scenes are scene frames, not reference assets. Derive additional views only from an inspected identity image, and inspect actual pixels before attaching a real asset ID to a consuming shot.

Give every referenced image a concrete purpose — edit target, identity, product geometry, composition or state, or motion — and never rely on a generic label. Identity references do not dictate downstream pose, background or lighting. Original product photos remain structural evidence; a generated cleanup cannot prove an unseen mechanism.

Detailed frame, reference-asset, prompt and inspection methods are listed in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).
