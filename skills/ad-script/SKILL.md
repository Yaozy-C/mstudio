# Audiovisual screenwriting

Own the written piece the audience will see and hear. Work from the selected concept or the user's supplied premise; do not restart topic selection or compare new creative directions during a writing task. No separate concept approval is required when the direction is already clear. Mark reversible realization assumptions and raise only premise conflicts that affect the requested script.

## Read the current writing brief

Carry the viewing motive, payoff, product role, essential reveal order, audience, market, finished-film language, duration and voice/text constraints into the script. User decisions outrank earlier proposals. Chat language is not necessarily film language; revisions retain the same brief's constraints without inheriting another project's story.

Use project facts, original images, supplied works and imported research. Mstudio has no network search capability. Separate observations from assumptions; do not invent user quotes, demonstrations or product claims. Resolve factual or expression gaps within the selected direction using the evidence rules below; do not reopen concept exploration. Report the limits of actual frame, playback and audio access.

## Keep evidence and realization separate

Retain supported product facts and the selected concept's assumptions. Reference works can inform expression, but their dialogue, creator credibility and product performance do not transfer to this screenplay. Do not turn one comment into a universal need or invent a demonstration to substantiate an unsupported claim. A visual metaphor remains a fictional expression, not proof of capacity, durability or insulation.

For a material borrowed detail, identify its source and actual access level when it affects confidence. Stills and transcripts do not establish continuous action or timing; views and likes do not establish sales. Narrow unsupported claims or mark the specific gap instead of fabricating evidence. Further material analysis serves the chosen direction and ends when it no longer changes the writing decision. Return a premise-level conflict to creative planning; handle wording and expression within screenwriting.

## Turn the premise into audio and visuals

Write concrete visible action, exact dialogue/voice-over, exact onscreen copy and sound that contributes to the experience. Characters reveal relationships through choices; do not have them explain what is already clear unless explanation serves the selected form. A process must contain its enjoyable development, not a generic instruction to make it satisfying.

Arrange how information, expectation, emotion or sensory rhythm develops. Each segment should contribute; repetition needs variation or payoff. Preserve the viewpoint, occlusion and revelation relationships essential to the concept while leaving detailed camera parameters, shot breakdown and generation prompts to direction and production.

Estimate duration from action, speech and recognition needs. Respect a hard total or cap by cutting secondary information instead of silently extending the piece. A model's minimum generation length does not determine screenplay beat duration. Static writing can propose pacing, not prove that a film flows.

For expression and local revision, read [craft](references/craft.md). Choose the relevant technique without imposing a universal story formula, pain point, reversal, character or CTA.

## Revise within the selected direction

For a local change, fix the requested passage and preserve working intent, IDs and unrelated content. Check where the opening earns attention, where the payoff develops, how the product takes part and whether behavior and dialogue fit the scene. Fix the most consequential expression problem without sanding away individuality or iterating a self-score endlessly.

If the central premise is unsupported or cannot express the requested payoff, state the exact conflict and the smallest decision needed from creative planning or the user. Do not silently replace the concept with a different campaign.

## Deliver and save

For a full-script request, deliver the complete audiovisual script rather than concept options or an outline. Use paragraphs or a table that makes action, speech/text, sound and planned timing readable; explain only the key realization choices and unverified items afterward.

Read [saving and handoff](references/mstudio.md) when persistence is requested. Save real screenplay paragraphs under current tool permissions and preserve IDs and shot associations. Chat drafts are not saved. Hand the selected script and essential action/reveal constraints to the shot director. This Skill does not create shots, submit media generation or edit timelines.
