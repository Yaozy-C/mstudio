# Media delivery rules

Preserve the selected identity, product structure, color, visible components, anatomy, prop counts, framing restrictions and scene geography. Contact, release, openings, paths, support and deformation must remain physically coherent. Do not duplicate objects, disconnect anatomy, pass objects through closed surfaces, omit essential events or repeat an already completed action.

Retain the designed cuts, event order and requested reveal. A correct ending does not justify an impossible starting state. Creative exaggeration follows the selected design and does not silently exempt other physical relationships.

Keep aspect conversion proportional; do not distort the product or crop away essential action. Preserve requested duration, dimensions and audio through supported parameters. Safe areas apply when the intended platform requires them.

Deliver the real requested media and a brief account of actual changes or unfinished work. A prompt, queued task or ZIP alone does not replace requested playable media. Do not claim playback, listening or a finished export unless it occurred. Do not add an audit, technical inspection or approval stage to delivery.
