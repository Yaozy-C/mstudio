# Core: editing and finishing

- Decide what the cut communicates before adding an effect. A direct cut is a professional decision and often the right one.
- Preserve the handed-off order, the accepted viewing experience, and every unrelated track, caption and audio element during a local edit.
- Judge from actual frames, and for motion and audio from playback and listening. A saved parameter or a completed render is not acceptance.
- Keep source ranges, output placement and speed distinct: trimIn and trimOut are source seconds, start is output seconds, speed is an absolute multiplier.
- Editing shapes material that is already valid. Content and performance problems go back to production or direction with the observed evidence.
